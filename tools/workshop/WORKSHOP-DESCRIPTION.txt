Arma Commander: Actual

An unofficial, openly vibecoded port of the classic Arma 3 mod Arma Commander to Arma Reforger: a hybrid RTS/FPS where a commander orders squads on the map and can seamlessly take over any soldier or gunner on the ground.

Standalone, no other mods required. Built for both solo test range play and dedicated multiplayer.

==================================================
KEY FEATURES
==================================================

TACTICAL RTS COMMAND (M or F9)
- Full command map with real-time intel feed, squad status, and radio reports.
- Select squads with left-click or drag-box; issue Move, Defend, Assault, and Queue waypoints.
- Movement modes: Combat (careful, using cover), Fast (road sprint), and Silent.
- Building Garrisons: order infantry to hold windows and doors inside real buildings.
- Stalled-Contact Flanking: pinned or stalled infantry automatically executes coordinated tactical doglegs against spotted enemies.
- Shared Commanding in Multiplayer: multiple commanders can coordinate on the same side with tagged orders and shared requisition pools.

FIRST-PERSON COMBAT POSSESSION (F7 / F8)
- Press F7 on any friendly soldier or map marker to immediately take first-person control.
- Gunner Drive: man any turret seat and steer the vehicle directly using standard W/A/S/D inputs while the AI driver cooperates.
- Press F8 at any time to return safely to your command unit.

COMBINED ARMS & SUPPORTS
- Mortar Teams: buy deployable 3-man mortar crews that pack up, travel, deploy, and execute ballistic fire missions.
- Forward Observers: buy FO teams with radios to spot targets and automatically call mortar fire missions.
- Battalion Artillery: call off-map howitzer battery barrages.
- Air Support: call CAS bomb runs (audible flyovers), loitering gunships, and door-gunner transport orbits.
- Air Lift: call off-map transport helicopters to pick up squads and fly them directly across the battlefield to designated landing zones.
- Logistics & Construction: deploy construction trucks to erect field hospitals, repair bays, ammo depots, fuel stations, and forward helipads.

PROCEDURAL SCENARIO GENERATOR & IN-GAME SETUP
- In-Game Setup Screen: configure battlefields, threat levels, pricing, AI commander squad caps, starting RP, and income before match start.
- Procedural Generator (Kolguyev & any map): generates balanced military bases, industrial centers, towns, and HQs based on map geography.
- Interactive Setup Map: preview planned base locations on the map and drag/reposition bases before starting.
- Everon & Arland Skirmish Sizes: choose between full island, island halves, or small localized flashpoints.

ENHANCED TACTICAL AI (BETTER BATTLE BUDDIES)
- Built-in BBB tactical system: small arms vs kinetic armor learning (AI stops wasting rifle ammo on medium armor), safe medic triage, door opening, and combat engagement priority.

==================================================
CONTROLS
==================================================
M or F9        : Open / Close Command Map
F7             : Possess soldier under crosshair or cursor
F8             : Return to Command Unit
F6 (in vehicle): Emergency passenger dismount
F5             : Move selected squad to crosshair / cursor
W / A / S / D  : Steer vehicle while operating a turret (Gunner Drive)
` (backtick)   : Native squad command menu (while possessing a squad leader)

Rebind keys anytime in Settings > Controls > AC: Actual.

==================================================
FACTION MODS & COMPATIBILITY
==================================================
AC: Actual supports any two factions a map lists, selected on the setup screen:
- Vanilla: US Army, Soviet Armed Forces, and FIA.
- RHS: Status Quo: RHS USAF and RHS AFRF are fully supported when RHS is loaded, featuring authentic squads, vehicles, CAS, and equipment.
- Ukraine Armed Forces RHS: supported when loaded alongside RHS (select a pair with UA).
- Compatible with ACE All in One and the All-in-One Command Menu.

Other faction mods: can be played if listed in the scenario's faction manager with standard Game Master catalogs (unfitted mods will use standard fallbacks).
