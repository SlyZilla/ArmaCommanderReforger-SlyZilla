ARMA COMMANDER: REFORGER - Arland test scenario
===============================================
An early fan remake of the Arma 3 "Arma Commander" mode: you're the US commander.
You order squads around on the map, jump into any of your soldiers, buy
reinforcements with RP, and take bases from an AI-run Soviet side.
This is an alpha build, so expect rough edges.

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Arma Reforger on Steam, installed and up to date (Windows PC).
- Nothing else. No Workshop download, and no Workbench.

HOW TO START
------------
1. Unzip anywhere, e.g. your Desktop. Keep the files together, and don't
   rename the "ArmaCommanderReforger" folder inside.
2. Close Arma Reforger if it's running.
3. Double-click  Play-Arland.cmd  (next to this guide).
   The game starts straight into the scenario (singleplayer and offline).
   The first load takes a while because the game compiles the mod's scripts.
   If Windows SmartScreen asks, choose "More info" > "Run anyway". It's a
   one-line script that starts the game with the mod loaded.
   If the game isn't found, it's installed somewhere unusual. Tell the person
   who sent you this.

THE SITUATION
-------------
You spawn at the US Airfield on the west side of Arland. Your forces nearby
are 1st Squad, 2nd Squad, a Weapons Team, a crewed truck (Truck 1) and a
crewed Humvee (Humvee 1). The Soviets hold the east side with their own AI
commander, which buys units, defends, and attacks your bases.

Bases (distances are from your spawn at the Airfield):
  Airfield .......... YOURS. Your HQ; you buy units here.
  Barracks .......... yours, 0.5 km south-east
  Harbor ............ neutral, 0.5 km north
  Farm .............. neutral, 0.9 km east
  Military Hill ..... neutral, 1.4 km east (worth extra)
  Lighthouse ........ neutral control point, 1.7 km south (no income)
  Ridge ............. neutral, 1.8 km south-east
  Heide ............. SOVIET HQ, 1.9 km east (Heide town)
  Arlberg ........... Soviet, 1.9 km south-east
  Heide Lighthouse .. Soviet control point, 2.3 km east (on the coast)
  Arlberg Harbor .... Soviet and HIDDEN until your troops get near it.
                      It's 2.5 km south-east, on the coast past Arlberg.

- To capture a base, get soldiers inside its ring and clear the enemies out.
  More soldiers capture it faster. Garrisoned bases have defenders that appear
  when you get close.
- Bases you own give RP (Requisition Points) every minute. You can deploy
  units you buy at any base you own that isn't under attack.
- There's no win screen yet. Play until one side owns the map.

CONTROLS
--------
F9 (or M)   Open or close the command map. No map item is needed.
F7          Take over the soldier you're aiming at. On the map, F7 takes over
            the marker under the cursor.
F8          Go back to your own commander body.
Y           Game Master (host/admin). This is a debug and "fix Arma" tool:
            unstick vehicles, delete stuck units, spawn stuff. Press Y again
            to return.
`  (backtick, the default command menu key)
            The normal Reforger squad orders. They only work while you're
            controlling a squad leader.

ON THE COMMAND MAP
------------------
- Left-click a squad marker to select it. Left-click empty ground to deselect.
- The blue panel on the right shows the selected squad:
    Combat / Fast / Silent  how the squad moves
    Defend, then right-click   defend that spot
    Get in, then right-click   board a vehicle near the click
    Get out / Unload       dismount the squad, or the passengers it carries
    Reinforce              refill missing soldiers (3 RP each). They appear
                           at your nearest base and then have to reach
                           the squad.
    Switch to squad        jump into that squad's leader
- RIGHT-CLICK on the map moves the selected squad there. A line shows each
  squad's current order.
- To toggle Defend or Get in off again, click the same button a second time.
- BUYING UNITS: click "Requisition" at the top of the panel, click a unit in
  the list, then left-click one of YOUR base markers on the map. The unit
  deploys there. Right-click cancels. Vehicles come with crews. The list shows
  the price and stock, and your RP is shown in the panel.
- Base rings: blue = yours, red = enemy, yellow = neutral.

TIPS
----
- Load a squad into Truck 1: select the squad, click "Get in", then right-click
  on the truck. Then move the TRUCK's crew, and the passengers ride along.
- You can fight in person too. Press F7 on any of your soldiers, then F8 to go
  back to your commander.
- Stuck AI or a flipped vehicle? Press Y (Game Master) and fix it.

KNOWN LIMITS (alpha)
--------------------
- Singleplayer only in this package. Multiplayer is built in but hasn't been
  tested yet.
- There's no artillery or air support yet, and no victory condition.
- Logs are in ArmaCommanderReforger\.local-game\logs if something breaks.
  Sending the newest console.log helps a lot.
