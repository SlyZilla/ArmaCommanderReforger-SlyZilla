0.5.10

Hotfix for 0.5.9: AI commanders that push to the end, garrisons that fall back, vehicles that can leave their base, and structures that sit on slopes. Works with Better Battle Buddies 0.1.8.

AI COMMANDERS

- The enemy HQ is the last objective, not the first. AIs no longer open a match by sending their spare squads into the enemy's spawn.
- An AI whose HQ is far from every other base now takes far empty bases by truck instead of sitting there all match.
- Squads at quiet bases far behind the front move up to the front, by truck or in hops of at most 3 km. New squads bought at a rear HQ go forward the same way.
- AIs finish a beaten enemy: attacks on the last bases, the HQ included, are no longer called off over and over.
- No more truck after truck: an AI buys only trucks that carry troops, and only when none is idle. Idle trucks spread out, at most two to a base.
- Unarmed trucks under fire drive away instead of their crews bailing out beside them.
- FOBs go at least 600 m from any base that could fall, so they keep working as a deploy point.
- Doctrine changes less often: only losing ground or heavy losses change it at once.
- A squad manning a roadblock's gun no longer counts as riding a vehicle, so its first grab isn't called off a second after it sets out.

FIXED

- Bought vehicles no longer appear in walled yards they can't drive out of (nearly every one at Saint-Philippe).
- A captured base's garrison and patrol fall back to their nearest base instead of vanishing.
- A roadblock's men no longer run back in front of your own truck in the checkpoint lane (a Reforger 1.9 dodge).
- Tents, bunkers, MG nests, checkpoints, towers and helipads lean with a slope (up to 5 degrees, towers 2.5) and sink a little into the hill, so their low side no longer stands on air. Walls and wire follow the ground along their length and stay upright.

BETTER

- FIA road ambushes set up 25-70 m back in cover, crouched and facing the road, and most bring a PKM on its tripod.
