## Faction mods (disclaimer)

AC: Actual plays any two factions a map lists, picked on the in-game setup screen. Our maps list
the game's US, USSR and FIA, plus RHS US and RHS Russia when RHS is loaded, and UA through Ukraine
Armed Forces RHS. Other faction mods are not guaranteed: a mod only works if the map lists it and
the mod sets up its factions the standard way (Game Master catalogs), and even then it may look
rough (mixed uniforms, a squad leader as your command unit, vanilla stand-ins for what it lacks).
Only RHS and UA are hand-fitted. Want a faction mod supported properly? Ask on GitHub:
https://github.com/propst-design/ACActual/issues
