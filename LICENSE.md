## Changes made

This is not a copy of the original files. The game mode was rebuilt for Arma
Reforger's engine (Enforce Script instead of SQF), following the design and logic
of the original: commanding squads from the map, taking over units, bases and
requisition points, requisition, and the AI commander (adapted from
`ai_strategic.sqf` and `ai_attack.sqf`). New features include supports,
command-unit respawn, helicopter transport, "gunner drives" and multiplayer
launchers. The command panel's look is an homage to the original's.
No Arma 3 assets (models, textures, sounds) are included.
